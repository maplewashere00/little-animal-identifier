Little Animal Identifier 🐾

Meet Cat Detective! Look for cats in a photo, or switch to the original animal identifier.

What should we look for?

Choose a photo to begin.

Cat Detective searches for cats using an object detector; it may miss real cats or mistake other objects for cats. Its scores are not calibrated accuracy percentages. A missing detection doesn't prove no cat is present. Your photo is processed in your browser, not uploaded to our server. Internet is required to download the AI libraries and model weights.
